An emotional 9:16 short film about a young African man who finds the courage to keep going after one more rejection.

Opening Hook

Intro — The Hook

One more no, and I was done.

Darkness. A match strikes. The flame lifts the word REJECTED out of a crumpled letter — and for a moment, his reflection in the rain-streaked glass decides whether to let it burn. The opening three seconds are built on motion, light change and a question the viewer has to answer.

Rendered from a single locked character design so the face, the rust-orange hoodie and the light source stay consistent across every shot of the film.
